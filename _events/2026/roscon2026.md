## See you in Toronto

I will be attending [ROSCon Global 2026](https://roscon.ros.org/2026/){: target="_blank"} in Toronto, Canada, from September 22 to 24, representing [Ouster](https://ouster.com/){: target="_blank"} and [Stereolabs](https://www.stereolabs.com/){: target="_blank"}.

ROSCon Global is the fifteenth annual international conference for the Robot Operating System community. Across three days, the program brings together workshops, technical talks, tutorials, demonstrations, informal Birds of a Feather sessions, and the exhibition hall: a practical place to share work, discuss the ecosystem, and meet the people building it.

## Event details

The conference will be held at the **Sheraton Centre Toronto**, 123 Queen Street West, Toronto, Ontario. The official program runs from Tuesday, September 22 through Thursday, September 24; workshops take place on the first day, followed by the main conference sessions and exhibition.

Toronto’s active robotics and AI community makes it a fitting setting for ROSCon Global. I am particularly looking forward to the sessions and conversations around ROS 2, perception, sensing, and deploying robotics systems beyond the lab.

## Ouster and Stereolabs

Ouster and Stereolabs now combine digital lidar, stereo cameras, AI compute, sensor fusion, and perception software in a unified sensing and perception platform. If you are attending, please come and say hello.
