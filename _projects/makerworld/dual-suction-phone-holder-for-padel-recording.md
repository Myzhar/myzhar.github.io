## Overview

Capture every rally, smash, and epic point with this specially designed **3D printed phone holder** for padel courts!
Built with **two powerful suction cups**, it locks firmly onto the glass walls, giving you a rock-steady angle for recording your matches.

✨ **Why you’ll love it:**

- **Super stable shots** – dual suction keeps your phone safe and steady.
- **Universal design** – fits most smartphones, no extra tools needed.
- **Quick & easy setup** – mount it in seconds, remove it just as fast.
- **Lightweight & portable** – toss it in your sports bag and you’re ready to go.

Whether you’re looking to **improve your game, share highlights**, or just keep memories of your best rallies, this holder makes recording effortless.

The picture shows several test versions I created before settling on the final design, which is printed on the right in **PLA** (red) and **ABS** (black). My first attempt used a spring mechanism to keep the jaws closed, but it proved to be quite challenging. I eventually opted for a simpler and more reliable solution: interlocking saw teeth.
