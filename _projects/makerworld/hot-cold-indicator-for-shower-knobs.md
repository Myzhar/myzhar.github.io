## Overview

**Hot? Cold? Just Right?** 🥶🔥🚿

Ever stepped into the shower and played a game of “Guess That Temperature”? Me too.

So I made this handy little *hot/cold* indicator to end the daily arctic surprise or accidental scalding.

These 3D-printed badges for the shower knob display the sun for hot and the snowflake for cold, because nobody wants to feel like a rotisserie chicken or a popsicle first thing in the morning.

Simple, colorful, and shower-friendly. Stick it near your shower knobs using a little glue or transparent silicone, and save your skin (literally).

🖨️ Printed on my Bambu with some juicy multicolor action.
👍 Your skin and your sanity will thank you.
