## Overview

### Summary

A simple holder for the STEREOLABS ZED Mini 3D camera:
[https://www.stereolabs.com/products/zed-2](https://www.stereolabs.com/products/zed-2){: target="_blank"}

The holder has two M3 holes to fix the camera to the case of a robot, and it has been designed to keep the middle of the stereo baseline in the middle of its base.

A mirrored model is provided to hold the camera upside down.

Four M1.7 holes can be used to fix the camera to the holder to give more stability.
